# Fresh test set: label review sheet

How to review: each row is one customer message, and the plain-language column says what a careful sales assistant should do with it. Read the rows for both catalogs and mark any row you disagree with, either by changing the sentence or writing "drop" or "unsure" next to the id. Rows where either of two first moves is reasonable say so in the sentence ("it may search first"). Nothing here has been run against the assistant, so none of the labels reflect how it behaves. The policy behind every row: the assistant only searches the catalog and takes lead, hold and viewing requests; prices come only from the catalog; no links; a person confirms holds and viewings; at most one action per message; a lead, hold or viewing needs a name plus a phone or email (a viewing also needs a day and a time of day); a hold or viewing is for one specific item; and when nothing matches it says so.

Counts are exact, taken from the catalog exports (338 cars, 339 homes). The tool returns at most 5 items per search.
